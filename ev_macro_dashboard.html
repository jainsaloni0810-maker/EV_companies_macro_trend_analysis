Key Macro Insight #1 — Inflation: A 1pp rise in CPI is associated with a +8.0pp increase in Tesla's YoY revenue growth (driven by product pricing power) but −5.8pp compression on EBITDA margins through input costs. Net effect is margin dilution at high inflation.
Key Macro Insight #2 — Interest Rates: The Fed rate coefficient on market cap growth is −0.242 (log-log). A 100bp rate hike reduces EV market cap growth by ~24%, consistent with duration-sensitive growth-stock valuation theory.
Key Macro Insight #3 — Lithium: The lithium price index peaked at 480 in 2022 (vs 100 in 2019) — a 380% surge. Battery packs represent ~40% of EV COGS. Normalisation to 145 by 2024 restores significant margin potential for vertically integrated players like BYD and Tesla.
